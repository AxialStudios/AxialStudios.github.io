Replicant

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 25, 2026 · Axial Studios

Replicant is a game where you guess which of two images is real and which is AI-generated. We keep data collection deliberately minimal:

You play under an anonymous account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number, and we do not collect your precise location. We record how you play (your guesses, response times, and progress) so we can show you your accuracy and stats, and show everyone the aggregate percentage of players who got each image right. We do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers. You can delete your data anytime from Profile, then Settings, then Delete account & data.

1. Information we collect

Account identifier. When you first open the app we create an anonymous account through our authentication provider, Supabase. This is a random identifier used to save your progress and link it to your subscription. It is not tied to your real-world identity.

Gameplay data.

  • The image pairs you were shown and your real vs. AI choices
  • Your response time per round
  • Timestamps of when you played
  • Collection progress and completions, daily-challenge results, and streaks

Optional onboarding information. During a short, skippable onboarding you may choose to tell us a coarse age range (for example, 18 to 24), a general region (for example, North America, not a precise location), and your self-rated familiarity with AI images. These are stored with your anonymous account to tailor and improve the experience and to understand, in aggregate, who plays. You can skip this step. We do not store precise location, IP-based location, or your exact birthdate.

Subscription and purchase status. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat. We receive whether you have an active subscription and its type. We do not receive or store your credit-card number or full payment details.

Technical data for app operation. Standard information needed to run the app and keep it secure, such as app version and basic request data handled by our backend and by Apple. We do not embed third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

2. How we use your information

  • To run the game and save your progress across sessions
  • To calculate your accuracy, streaks, and personal analytics
  • To compute the aggregate percentages shown after each round, from pooled, non-identifying vote counts
  • To provide and manage subscriptions and restore purchases
  • To maintain, secure, debug, and improve the app and its content
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Legal bases for processing (EEA and UK users)

Where GDPR applies, we process data on the basis of: performance of a contract (to provide the app and your account), our legitimate interests (to improve and secure the app and prevent abuse), your consent where required (for example, optional profile info), and legal obligation where applicable.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us operate the app, under agreements that limit their use of it:

  • Supabase for authentication, database, and backend hosting
  • Cloudflare R2 for image storage and delivery
  • RevenueCat for subscription management
  • Apple for in-app purchases and app distribution

We may also disclose information if required by law, to enforce our terms, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of our users or the public.

5. Data retention

Aggregate vote data is retained as part of the game's core statistics; it powers the public percentages and is not used to profile you personally. Your personal progress and account data are retained while your account exists. When you delete your account, your writable personal data is removed and your anonymous session is ended.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: Profile, then Settings, then Delete account & data.
  • Manage your subscription or restore purchases: in the app or in your Apple ID settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example, the EEA and UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA and CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these, contact us at hello@axialstudios.app. We do not discriminate against you for exercising your rights.

7. Children's privacy

Replicant is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. In onboarding we ask your age range; if you indicate you are under 13, we do not save your onboarding profile. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your data, including row-level security on our database so users can only access their own records, and encryption in transit. No method of transmission or storage is fully secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. International data transfers

Our service providers may process data in countries other than yours. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version with an updated effective date and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.

11. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data: hello@axialstudios.app, Axial Studios. We use email as our contact method and do not publish a mailing address.
