This page is support for Replicant, the real-vs-AI photo guessing game by Axial Studios. Most questions are answered below. If you need anything else, email us and we will get back to you, usually within two business days.

Contact: hello@axialstudios.app

Manage your subscription

Cancel or change your plan. Subscriptions are billed through Apple, so you manage them in your Apple ID settings: open the Settings app, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then choose Replicant. You can also open your Apple subscriptions directly.

Restore a purchase. If you reinstalled the app or switched devices, open Replicant, go to Profile, then Settings, then Restore purchases.

Free trial. If your plan includes a free trial, Apple reminds you before it ends, and you can cancel anytime before then in your Apple subscription settings with no charge.

Your account and data

Delete your account and data. In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account & data. This removes your personal data and ends your anonymous session.

Save your progress across devices. In Profile, then Settings, you can link your Apple ID so your streaks and stats follow you if you reinstall or switch phones.

For how we handle your data, see our Privacy Policy.

How the game works

Each round shows two images side by side. One is a real photograph, one was generated by AI. Tap the one you think is real, then see if you were right along with the tells hidden in the image. Play the Daily, work through Collections, and track your accuracy over time.

Report a problem or share an idea

Found an image you think is labeled wrong, hit a bug, or have a feature idea? The fastest way to reach us is the in-app form: Profile, then Settings, then Send feedback. You can also email hello@axialstudios.app.
